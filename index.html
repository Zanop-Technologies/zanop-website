Welcome traveller to Zanop Technologies, we were founded in the year of 2022 by John Adman. Zanop OS was supposed to be a fictional company project but before Zanop OS had a github originazation, Zanop OS developed fictional products such as the Zanop A Series smartphone which was deeply inspired by the Samsung A Series.

Traveller, Zanop OS isnt just a linux distribution, this Operating System has its own kernel which powers Zanop Desktop OS, Zanop Television OS, Zanop Mobile OS, and all future variants of the Zanop Kernel.
